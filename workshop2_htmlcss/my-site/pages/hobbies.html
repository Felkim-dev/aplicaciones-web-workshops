Videogames

Video games have been one of my favorite hobbies for as long as I can remember. Lately, I've been spending most of my free time in Elden Ring, exploring the Lands Between at my own pace, fighting bosses that take dozens of attempts, and slowly piecing together the game's lore instead of rushing through the main story. What I enjoy most about games like this is the sense of discovery — finding a hidden area, an optional boss, or a shortcut that completely changes how a fight feels.

Beyond Elden Ring, I like games that reward patience and experimentation, since that mindset carries over nicely into how I approach programming projects: try something, fail, adjust, and try again.

Exercise

Exercise is the other hobby that keeps me balanced outside of classes and coding. I try to work out regularly, mixing strength training with some cardio, mostly because it helps me clear my head after a long day of studying or debugging code.

It's also become a good way to measure progress in something concrete and immediate — unlike a semester-long project, I can see week-to-week improvement in how much I can lift or how a workout feels, which keeps me motivated to stay consistent.
